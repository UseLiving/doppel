# DOPPEL ($DOPPEL)

Every coin that crosses the line gets a ghostly double.

**Live site:** https://useliving.github.io/doppel/  
**X:** https://x.com/UseDoppel

## What it does
- Streams every new pump.fun launch live and tracks each coin's market cap trade by trade
- Spots coins that cross the pair threshold ($25k by default, adjustable) and queues a pair
- Backfills recent pump.fun coins already above the line from DexScreener
- Bot log shows spots, pairs, failed launches, migrations and fee burns
- Click any coin for its live chart, trades and links to pump.fun, DexScreener and Solscan
- Halloween edition: bats, fog, ghosts and jump scares (toggle in the header)

Single self-contained `index.html`. Live data comes from the public PumpPortal stream and DexScreener API.

> Pair launches and burns shown on the site are simulated. Not financial advice.
