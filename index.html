01 · watchEvery launch is tracked

The scanner subscribes to each new pump.fun coin the moment it's created and follows its market cap trade by trade.

02 · spotCrossing the line

When a coin crosses the pair threshold ($25k by default), it gets spotted and queued for a pair.

03 · pairA double, paired to $DOPPEL

The bot launches a copy of the coin with $DOPPEL as its quote instead of SOL. Coins that can't be paired are skipped and logged.

04 · burnFees buy and burn

Creator fees from every pair are claimed, swapped to $DOPPEL and burned. Tiny fee balances are skipped until they're worth it.

Source coins and market caps come from live pump.fun and DexScreener data. Pair launches and burns on this page are simulated.Happy Halloween. Not financial advice.
